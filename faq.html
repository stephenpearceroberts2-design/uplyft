Getting Started

How long does onboarding take?

Typically 48 to 72 hours from your initial brief to a live campaign. We handle the entire setup: agents, AI, and portal, then walk your team through it before anything goes live.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short brief: your target sector, seniority level, and region, whether you're sourcing candidates, targeting new clients, or both. That's the only input required from your side. We configure everything else.

Do we need to do anything technical?

No. There's nothing to install or configure yourself. We build and run the entire system, and train your team on the portal so everyone can see campaign status, progress, and analytics from day one.

Can we use it for candidate sourcing and new business at the same time?

Yes. Both engines run on the same underlying platform. Many agencies start with one and add the other once they've seen it working.

How It Works

What does the AI actually say to candidates or prospects?

It holds a genuine, context-aware conversation, not a scripted message. It's trained specifically on recruitment dialogue, reads context and intent, and responds the way a skilled consultant would. You can read every conversation in the portal.

Will this put our LinkedIn account at risk?

The agent is built to mimic natural human behaviour: pacing, timing, and volume, so activity stays within platform tolerances rather than firing off requests at a fixed, robotic rate. For the full detail on how this works, see our Trust & Compliance page.

How many connections does it send per day?

20 or more per agent, per day, running continuously in the background rather than in one manual burst.

How will we know when someone's ready to talk?

You'll get an instant notification the moment a candidate or prospect is ready for a call or asks for more information. Everything also sits in your portal: who's been contacted, and how each conversation is progressing.

Data & Oversight

Where does your candidate and client data come from?

Nowhere but the conversation itself. We don't scrape or buy lists. Every contact in your portal is someone who accepted a connection and engaged directly with your outreach.

Is anyone reviewing what the AI is doing?

Yes. Your dedicated account lead reviews performance weekly. This isn't a fully unsupervised system left to run without anyone checking in on it. For more on this, see our Trust & Compliance page.

Pricing

How much does it cost?

£299 per agent, per month. Every seat includes the full platform: setup, AI, portal, and a dedicated account lead. No feature gates or hidden tiers.

Can we run more than one agent?

Yes. Add as many agents as you need across your team, at the same per-seat price. Agencies running 25 or more agents move to a custom enterprise arrangement.

Which sectors does this work best for?

Specialist agencies where a placement or a new client is worth real money, typically £10k or more. See our Who It's For page for the sectors we know best.
