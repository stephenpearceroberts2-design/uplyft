Trust & Compliance

How this actually
works, plainly.

Automating outreach on your agency's LinkedIn account is a reasonable thing to be cautious about. Here's exactly what the system does, in plain terms, with nothing glossed over.

Account Safety

Built to behave
like a person,
not a script.

The agent runs on your agency's own LinkedIn account, logged in as your consultant, not through a third-party API or a scraper. It sends connection requests and messages at a pace and pattern designed to resemble how a person actually uses LinkedIn: varied timing, sensible daily volume, and pauses, rather than firing off requests at a fixed, mechanical rate.

We can't guarantee how any third-party platform will treat any account. What we can tell you is exactly what the agent does, and that it's built specifically to operate within the kind of usage pattern a genuine, active user would show.

Human-paced activity

Connection requests and messages go out with varied timing and volume, not at a constant, robotic rate.

Daily limits, not maximum throughput

Around 20 connections per agent, per day, well inside typical LinkedIn usage rather than pushed to the platform's outer limits.

One account, one identity

The agent operates as your consultant on your consultant's own account. It doesn't create fake profiles or borrow someone else's identity.

No scraping, no bought lists

Every candidate and prospect in your portal is someone who accepted a connection request and engaged with the conversation directly. Nobody's details arrive from a database we didn't build a relationship with.

Contact details, given not taken

Email addresses and phone numbers are only recorded when the person shares them in the conversation. The AI is instructed never to promise a call or email unless that channel has actually been offered.

Visible to your team only

Everything the agent and AI do lives in your portal: your account, your data, your team's visibility.

Data & Consent

Nothing in your
pipeline that didn't
opt in.

We don't scrape LinkedIn profiles, buy contact lists, or import third-party databases. Every record in your portal came from someone accepting an outreach request and taking part in a real conversation.

If you'd like more detail on how a specific piece of candidate or prospect data was captured, your account lead can walk you through the exact conversation it came from.

Human Oversight

A person checks
this. Every week.

This isn't a system we switch on and walk away from. Your dedicated account lead reviews campaign performance and conversation quality on a weekly basis, flags anything that looks off, and is who you speak to if you ever want to see exactly what's been said on your behalf.

Full conversation visibility

Every message sent and received is readable in your portal, in full, in order. There's no hidden layer between what the AI said and what you can see.

Plainly Stated

What we don't do.

i. We don't scrape LinkedIn profiles or buy third-party contact lists.
ii. We don't create fake profiles or misrepresent who's messaging someone.
iii. We don't let the AI promise a phone call or email unless the person has already offered that contact method.
iv. We don't push connection or message volume beyond what looks like ordinary, human LinkedIn use.
v. We don't run campaigns unsupervised. A dedicated account lead reviews activity every week.
